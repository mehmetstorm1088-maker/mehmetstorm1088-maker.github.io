Website terms

Information for families, with a clear distinction between this website and the publisher’s private tools.

Effective date: 15 October 2026

1. About BlinkQuest Lab

This website introduces the BlinkQuest Lab publishing project and a planned collection of original English animal cartoon stories for ages 4–7. Story ideas are illustrative development plans, not a claim that those children’s videos are already published. The linked YouTube channel also contains earlier visual-puzzle content.

2. Using this website

You may browse the pages and follow the channel links for personal information and viewing. This website does not offer accounts, purchases, competitions, a contact form or a visitor upload service. Do not use it to interfere with the hosting service or misrepresent an association with the publisher.

3. Families and external video services

Parents and caregivers decide which content and viewing settings suit their children. Links to YouTube open a separate service. Use of YouTube is governed by YouTube’s Terms of Service, and its data handling is described in Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not operate YouTube or control its recommendations, advertisements, accounts or availability.

4. Stories, illustrations and names

Original website illustrations and text belong to the publisher unless otherwise stated. Public availability does not grant permission to repost the stories, copy the illustrations for commercial use or impersonate the channel. Contact the publisher through the channel for reuse requests. Third-party service names remain the property of their respective owners.

5. Private publisher workflow

The BlinkQuest Lab Google-connected publishing workflow is for the adult channel owner’s administration of their own YouTube channel. It is not a child-facing service and is not offered for visitors to sign in or authorize. Upload permissions and the handling of the owner’s authorization data are explained in the Privacy Policy. No visitor Google credentials should be entered into this website.

6. Accuracy and availability

We aim to keep the site accurate and clear, but story plans, publishing dates and website features may change. External links and videos can become unavailable. The site is provided as an informational resource, without a promise of uninterrupted availability or a specific educational outcome. Nothing in these terms removes rights that applicable law gives you.

7. Contact and updates

Contact options, if available, are on the YouTube channel. No separate public email or address is supplied here. These terms may be updated when the project changes; the effective date identifies the current version.
