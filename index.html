How this website handles information ↗
  1. Clear, friendly English

    Spoken narration and simple visual storytelling, with a little time to follow along.

  2. The joke is kind

    Funny situations and animal mix-ups, rather than making someone the target.

  3. A small thoughtful moment

    Room for sharing, patience and friendship, without a big lecture.

  4. Grown-ups stay in the loop

    No child accounts, Google sign-in, forms or purchases on this website. YouTube opens as a separate service.
